# Nordesthetics Clinic — High-Performance Bespoke Digital Flagship (Sample 2)

A bespoke luxury digital flagship crafted for **Nordesthetics Clinic** (Kaunas, Lithuania) — Europe's premier plastic and cosmetic surgery clinic.

## Visual Highlights & Transfusion
- **Architecture:** Zero-slop bespoke editorial layout with tactile aesthetic medicine and clinical luxury.
- **Copy Adaptation:** 100% adapted copy for European plastic surgery (Tummy Tuck, Mommy Makeover, Breast Surgery, Facelifts, Rhinoplasty, Liposuction).
- **Surgeon Credentials:** Featuring Senior Plastic Surgeons (Dr. Jonas Tutkus, Dr. Simonas Kaupas, Dr. Kamile Tijunele, Dr. Karolis Bauzys) with 15+ years experience and ISO 9001 certified surgical suites.
- **Social Proof:** Integrated 2,400+ Trustpilot & Google Reviews, 45,000+ patient procedures, and authentic European patient travel transformation stories.
- **Motion & Performance:** Native CSS `linear(...)` harmonic oscillator easing curves, marquee tickers, and zero-dependency view transitions.
- **Analytics:** Integrated Vercel Web Analytics.
