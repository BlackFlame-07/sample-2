Who it’s for /

Is it right for you?

Whitening works on natural teeth with healthy enamel and gums. We check both before we start.

Stains from coffee, tea or wine

Surface and deeper stains from food and drink usually respond very well to peroxide whitening.

Healthy natural teeth

We check for decay and gum problems first, because whitening works best on sound enamel.

Before veneers or bonding

Whitening first lets us match any new restorations to your brighter shade.

Not ideal for

Crowns, veneers and fillings do not change colour, and whitening is not recommended during pregnancy. We will tell you honestly if it is not right for you.

Step by step / What happens during treatment

Every step is explained before it happens, and you can pause at any point.

  1. 01

    Shade check

    We record your starting shade with a shade guide and a photo, so you can see the change for yourself.

    10 min

  2. 02

    Protection

    Your gums are covered with a protective barrier and your lips and cheeks are gently held back.

    10 min

  3. 03

    Whitening gel

    Professional-strength gel is applied in timed cycles while your dentist checks for any sensitivity.

    35 min

  4. 04

    Result and trays

    We compare shades, take a 3D scan for your custom take-home trays and talk through how to keep the result.

    5 min

Results and aftercare /

What to expect afterwards

Most patients see a clear change after one session. The shade settles over about 48 hours and typically lasts one to three years, depending on diet and habits. Top-up gel in your custom trays keeps it fresh.

Aftercare

  • For 48 hours, avoid strongly coloured food and drink such as coffee, red wine, curry and berries.

  • Some sensitivity for a day or two is normal. A toothpaste for sensitive teeth helps.

  • Avoid smoking, which quickly re-stains freshly whitened enamel.

  • Use your custom trays for a top-up night or two every few months.
