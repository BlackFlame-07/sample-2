FAQ /

Questions, answered

Still unsure about something? Call the front desk on +44 203 949 5522 and we’ll talk it through.

Lithuania operates under strict EU healthcare directives with world-class surgeons who train across the UK, Germany, and Switzerland. Lower operating overhead allows Nordesthetics to offer private hospital care at 50% to 60% less than UK prices with all-inclusive packages.

All surgical fees, surgeon consultations, general anesthesia, private hospital overnight stay with meals, dedicated 24/7 English-speaking nursing care, personal airport & clinic transfers, compression garments, and fit-to-fly certificates are included.

Most patients stay 5 to 7 days in Lithuania. Your surgeon performs an in-person follow-up check-up, removes stitches if needed, and issues a medical Fit-to-Fly certificate prior to your departure.

Tell us when you book. We'll schedule a longer appointment, explain each step before it happens and agree on a stop signal. Headphones, weighted blankets and gentle sedation are available.

Treatments over $500 can be spread across 6 or 12 months at 0% interest. Your estimate shows both the full price and the monthly amount.
